### D. Answer the questions.

My name is Martin. I went to Beijing four years ago, but couldn't visit the Great Wall. I always wanted to see it but our tour guide didn't take us there because it was winter. Luckily, I visited the Great Wall of China as part of my Asia tour last June.

#### Choose the perfect time

I booked a private tour and had a wonderful experience because the tour guide was nice and friendly. He also spoke English well. He picked me up from the hotel at 6:00 am as I wanted to avoid (避开) the tourist traffic. It took us two hours to get to Mutianyu. I was surprised to feel the fresh air of Beijing.
