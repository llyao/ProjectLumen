# 朵朵英语阅读信心周计划

## 本周目标

这一周只练一个核心能力：**从短文中找到答案依据**。

不追求难题，不要求逐句翻译，也不提前学习大量语法。每天完成20分钟即可，目标是让朵朵逐渐体会到：阅读题可以通过方法做对。

文中的英文人物、学校和活动均为虚构练习内容，不代表朵朵的真实学校或经历。

本周希望看到三个小进步：

- 每道题都能在原文中画出依据。
- 做题时不留空白。
- 第七天重做错题时，比第一次多做对至少2题。

## 阅读五步法

记住口诀：**先看题，圈关键词；回原文，画依据；比意思，再选择。**

1. **先看题目：** 圈出人物、时间、地点、数字和动作。
2. **回到原文：** 寻找与关键词相同或意思相近的句子。
3. **画出依据：** 每道题必须在原文中画出对应内容。
4. **比较选项：** 注意选项可能会换一种说法，不一定照抄原文。
5. **最后检查：** 检查人物、时间、地点有没有看错，并确认没有空题。

### 遇到不认识的词怎么办

1. 在生词下面画一个小三角，暂时不要查词。
2. 如果题目没有问到这个词，就继续阅读。
3. 如果题目问到了，就在原文中找到含有这个词的完整句子，再和选项逐一比较。
4. 全部答完后再看文末的“挑战词核对区”。

### 常见提问词

| 提问词 | 题目在问什么 | 重点寻找 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Who` | 谁 | 人名、人物身份 |
| `When` | 什么时候 | 时间、日期、星期 |
| `Where` | 在哪里 | 地点、房间、城市 |
| `What` | 什么、做什么 | 动作或事物 |
| `Why` | 为什么 | `because`及原因 |
| `How` | 怎样 | 方法、交通工具 |
| `How long` | 多长时间 | 分钟、小时、天数 |
| `How many` | 多少 | 数字和数量 |

## 每天固定20分钟

| 环节 | 时间 | 具体做法 |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| 看题 | 2分钟 | 圈出题目关键词 |
| 阅读 | 6分钟 | 通读短文，不逐词翻译 |
| 答题 | 7分钟 | 先画原文依据，再选择答案 |
| 订正 | 3分钟 | 找出错题依据，说出错因 |
| 记录 | 2分钟 | 填写打卡表，确认没有空题 |

## 第1天：我的学校生活

**目标：** 学会寻找时间、地点、科目和课后活动。争取做对4题以上。

### My School Day

Hello, I'm Amy. I study at Green Middle School. My school starts at eight o'clock. My favourite subject is English because it is interesting. At noon, I have lunch with my classmates in the school canteen. Classes end at half past three. After school, I usually play badminton with my friend Lucy.

### 选择正确答案

1. When does Amy's school start?

   A. At 7:30.  
   B. At 8:00.  
   C. At 8:30.

2. What is Amy's favourite subject?

   A. English.  
   B. Maths.  
   C. Art.

3. What does Amy usually do after school?

   A. She reads books.  
   B. She plays football.  
   C. She plays badminton.

4. Where does Amy have lunch?

   A. In the school canteen.
   B. At home.
   C. In a restaurant.

5. Who plays badminton with Amy?

   A. Her teacher.
   B. Lucy.
   C. Her classmates.

完成后检查：五道题是否都画出了原文依据？不认识`canteen`也能否通过原词定位选出答案？

## 第2天：星期一课程表

**目标：** 学会寻找课程时间和原因。

### Ben's Monday

Ben is a Grade Six student. On Monday, he has English at 8:15 and Maths at 9:05. After the morning break, he has Science in the laboratory. He likes Science best because he can do experiments with his classmates. His classes end at 3:30. Then he goes to the football club.

### 选择正确答案

1. Which grade is Ben in?

   A. Grade Six.  
   B. Grade Five.  
   C. Grade Seven.

2. When does Ben have Maths?

   A. At 8:15.  
   B. At 9:05.  
   C. At 9:50.

3. Why does Ben like Science best?

   A. Because he can do experiments.  
   B. Because he can sing songs.  
   C. Because he can play football.

4. Where does Ben go after classes?

   A. To the football club.  
   B. To the library.  
   C. To the art room.

5. When do Ben's classes end?

   A. At 3:00.
   B. At 3:15.
   C. At 3:30.

完成后检查：`Why`题是否找到了`because`后面的内容？

## 第3天：学校阅读社团

**目标：** 学会从通知中寻找日期、地点和要求。

### Happy Reading Club

Our Reading Club is open every Tuesday and Thursday from 4:00 to 4:40 p.m. We meet in Room 205 of the school library. Please bring one book you like. We read for twenty minutes and then talk about the book. The librarian, Ms Wang, helps us choose books. To join the club, tell your English teacher before Monday.

### 选择正确答案

1. When is the Reading Club open?

   A. Every Monday and Friday.  
   B. Every Tuesday and Thursday.  
   C. Every Wednesday and Friday.

2. Where do the students meet?

   A. In the classroom.  
   B. In the art room.  
   C. In Room 205 of the school library.

3. What should each student bring?

   A. One book.  
   B. One notebook.  
   C. One picture.

4. Who should students tell before Monday?

   A. Their parents.  
   B. Ms Wang.  
   C. Their English teacher.

5. How long do the students read?

   A. For ten minutes.
   B. For twenty minutes.
   C. For forty minutes.

完成后检查：是否圈出了星期、房间号和人物？

## 第4天：上海博物馆之行

**目标：** 学会寻找交通方式、活动顺序和感受。

### A Day at the Museum

Last Saturday, Jane visited Shanghai Natural History Museum with her parents. They left home at 8:30 and went there by underground. At the entrance, they got a map. Jane saw many animals and visited an exhibition about insects. She liked the dinosaurs best. At noon, the family had lunch in a park near the museum. They returned home at 4:00. Jane was tired but happy.

### 选择正确答案

1. When did Jane visit the museum?

   A. Last Friday.  
   B. Last Saturday.  
   C. Last Sunday.

2. How did the family go to the museum?

   A. By bus.  
   B. By car.  
   C. By underground.

3. What did Jane like best?

   A. The dinosaurs.  
   B. The insects.  
   C. The park.

4. How did Jane feel at the end of the day?

   A. Angry and tired.  
   B. Tired but happy.  
   C. Sad but excited.

5. Where did the family have lunch?

   A. In the museum.
   B. At home.
   C. In a park near the museum.

完成后检查：`How`在第2题和第4题中分别询问什么？

## 第5天：新同学的第一天

**目标：** 在直接信息题之外，尝试一道简单主旨题。

Leo felt nervous on his first day at Sunrise Middle School. At lunchtime, he sat alone because he did not know anyone. Mia noticed him and invited him to sit with her friends. Later, Leo could not find the art room, so Mia showed him the way. After class, she took him to the basketball club. Leo felt happy. He thought his new school was a friendly place.

词语提示：`alone`独自；`show the way`指路。其他生词先不查。

### 选择正确答案

1. Why did Leo sit alone at lunchtime?

   A. Because he did not like lunch.  
   B. Because he did not know anyone.  
   C. Because he wanted to read.

2. Who helped Leo?

   A. Mia.  
   B. His teacher.  
   C. His parents.

3. Where did Mia take Leo after class?

   A. To the art room.  
   B. To the library.  
   C. To the basketball club.

4. What is the best title for the passage?

   A. A Long School Day  
   B. A Helpful New Friend  
   C. An Interesting Art Room

5. How did Leo feel after Mia helped him?

   A. Happy.
   B. Angry.
   C. Tired.

完成后检查：主旨题的答案能否概括整篇文章，而不是只概括一句话？

## 第6天：校园开放日

**目标：** 综合练习人物、时间、地点、顺序和要求。

### School Open Day

Dear Lucy,

Our School Open Day is this Friday. Here is the schedule. Please come to the school gate, the main entrance, at 1:30 p.m. My teacher and I will meet you there. First, we will visit our classrooms and the science lab. At 2:30, we will watch an art show in the school hall. Please bring a notebook, but do not bring any food. The Open Day will end at 4:00 p.m.

See you on Friday!

Mary

词语提示：`Open Day`开放日；`school hall`学校礼堂。

### 选择正确答案

1. When is the School Open Day?

   A. This Thursday.  
   B. This Friday.  
   C. This Saturday.

2. Where will Mary meet Lucy?

   A. At the school gate.  
   B. In the science lab.  
   C. In the school hall.

3. What will they do at 2:30?

   A. Visit the classrooms.  
   B. Have some food.  
   C. Watch an art show.

4. What should Lucy bring?

   A. Some food.  
   B. A notebook.  
   C. A picture.

5. When will the Open Day end?

   A. At 3:30 p.m.  
   B. At 4:00 p.m.  
   C. At 4:30 p.m.

完成后检查：五道题是否都能指出原文中的对应句？

## 第7天：只复习，不学新内容

1. 用5分钟朗读本周最喜欢的一篇短文。
2. 用10分钟重新完成本周全部错题，不看原答案。
3. 用3分钟说出阅读五步法。
4. 用2分钟填写“本周进步记录”。

如果重做后比第一次多对2题，就已经完成本周目标。

## 家长陪伴方法

朵朵做题时，家长只需要问：

1. “这道题问的是人物、时间、地点，还是事情？”
2. “原文哪句话告诉你的？”
3. “另外几个选项为什么不符合原文？”

请避免逐句翻译全文，也不要在她第一次选错时立即报答案。先请她回到原文再找一次。

可以具体表扬：

- “今天每道题都画出了依据。”
- “这次没有空题。”
- “你自己发现人物看错了，并且改对了。”
- “文章变长了，你还是完成了。”

### 难度调整规则

- 连续两天达到80%以上：保持当前方法，下一周每篇增加约10至20个词。
- 单日低于60%：不批评，也不增加题量；第二天先重做当天错题，再完成新短文。
- 能选对但找不到原文依据：暂不算真正掌握，需要重新定位。
