## 基础练习

### 二、根据中文写出英语词组或句子。

**2.** 不同的学科
＿＿＿＿＿＿＿＿＿＿＿＿＿＿＿＿

**朵朵原答案：** different different from（前一处 different 被划去）

**正确答案：** different subjects

**错误解析：** different 意为“不同的”，后接可数名词复数 subjects，构成“不同的学科”。different from 表示“与……不同”，不是本题所需词组。照片订正中 subject 的词尾不清，标准表达应有复数 s。

**照片中的订正记录：** different subject（照片中红笔如此书写，词尾是否有复数 s 不清）

### 三、选择合适的词填入空格中。

**8.** Please ＿＿＿＿＿＿ your computers first and make sure it works. (open/turn on)

**朵朵原答案：** open

**正确答案：** turn on

**错误解析：** 开启电脑等电器用 turn on；open 常用于打开门、书或文件。原题 computers 为复数，后面的 it works 存在单复数不一致，通常应写 they work；本题空格仍填 turn on，原题另行保留。

**照片中的订正记录：** turn on

### 五、用所给单词的适当形式填空。

**3.** We give computer ＿＿＿＿＿＿ by putting a program into it. (instruct)

**朵朵原答案：** instruct

**正确答案：** instructions

**错误解析：** give 后需要名词作宾语，instruct 是动词“指示”，应变为名词 instruction；一组操作指令通常用复数 instructions。原题 give computer 的表达缺少冠词或复数标记，宜核对原卷，但不影响空格词形。

**照片中的订正记录：** instructions

**5.** I studied ＿＿＿＿＿＿ last semester. (France)

**朵朵原答案：** France

**正确答案：** French

**错误解析：** 这里表达学习一门语言，应使用 French（法语）。France 表示国家“法国”，不能表示所学语言。

**照片中的订正记录：** French

**6.** A lost of people lost their ＿＿＿＿＿＿ in the war. (life)

**朵朵原答案：** life

**正确答案：** lives

**错误解析：** 句中 people 和 their 指许多人，lose their lives 表示“失去生命”；life 变复数时把 fe 改为 ves，写作 lives。原题 A lost of people 疑为 A lot of people 的笔误，保留原题并提示核对。

**照片中的订正记录：** lives

### 六、时态填空题。

**1.** She ＿＿＿＿＿＿ to her penfriend once a month. (write)

**朵朵原答案：** is writing

**正确答案：** writes

**错误解析：** once a month 表示每月一次的习惯，使用一般现在时。主语 She 是第三人称单数，write 加 s；is writing 表示正在写，不能表达这里的日常频率。

**3.** Tim ＿＿＿＿＿＿ my schoolbag just now. (not see)

**朵朵原答案：** doesn't see

**正确答案：** didn't see

**错误解析：** just now 表示“刚才”，使用一般过去时。否定式为 didn't 加动词原形 see，不能用一般现在时 doesn't see。

**5.** Joyce ＿＿＿＿＿＿ with her students in class during the break this morning. (talk)

**朵朵原答案：** talks

**正确答案：** talked

**错误解析：** 句意是 Joyce 今天上午课间与学生交谈，按已经发生的事情使用一般过去时，talk 的过去式为 talked。talks 是一般现在时第三人称单数，常用于习惯或事实。

## 阅读训练

### 阅读训练 B　Answer the questions.

Rick Hansen was an active person and loved fishing very much. One day, when returning in a truck from a fishing trip, he had an accident and hurt his spine（脊柱）. Unluckily, this 15-year-old young man awoke to find that he had lost the use of his legs. However, he didn't give up. Many months later, he began to compete（比赛）in wheelchair sports. Then, at the age of 27, he decided to wheel around the world to raise money for the spinal research（研究）. His trip took him through 34 countries around the world between 1985 and 1987. And he wheeled 24,901.55 miles, which is equal（相等的）to the distance（距离）around the Earth.

During the trip, bad weather and roads often provided（给予）many difficulties for Rick, but he continued to push himself to finish the trip. Even mountains could not stop Rick. He wheeled himself up the Rocky Mountains and several other major mountains in the world. He even wheeled himself along the Great Wall of China! “Never Give Up Your Dreams” was his motto（格言）.

**正确答案：** He lost the use of his legs.

**错误解析：** 问题问事故给 Rick 带来的结果。第一段写他醒来后发现双腿失去功能，回答需抓住 lost the use of his legs。原记录为未作答。
