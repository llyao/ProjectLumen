## 基础练习

### 三、选择题

**14.** Mr Wang wants to know ______ about the clubs.
A. an information
B. many informations
C. a lot of informations
D. more information

**朵朵原答案：** A（照片中手写答案）

**正确答案：** D. more information

**错误解析：** information 是不可数名词，不能用 an，也不能加复数；more information 表示“更多信息”。

**15.** What ______ do you want to know about the school?
A. too
B. either
C. also
D. else

**朵朵原答案：** B. either（照片中手写答案）

**正确答案：** D. else

**错误解析：** 疑问词后表示“还、另外”用 else；either 主要用于否定句或疑问句中的“也”。

**16.** ______ teaches you English this term? - Mrs Miller.
A. Who
B. What
C. Who does
D. What does

**朵朵原答案：** C. Who does（照片中手写答案）

**正确答案：** A. Who

**错误解析：** Who 在句中作主语，直接用“Who teaches...?”，不需要助动词 does。

**18.** ______ word did you underline, post or poster, Jenny? - Oh, it was poster.
A. Which
B. What
C. How many
D. How much

**朵朵原答案：** B. What（照片中手写答案）

**正确答案：** A. Which

**错误解析：** 在 post 和 poster 两个确定选项中选择，用 Which；What 不强调有限范围内的选择。

### 四、用单词的适当形式填空

**1.** In fact, everyone ______ (have) his or her own study habit.

**朵朵原答案：** have

**正确答案：** has

**错误解析：** everyone 是不定代词，作第三人称单数主语，谓语用 has。

**5.** Sadly, ten people lost their ______ (life) in the big fire.

**朵朵原答案：** life

**正确答案：** lives

**错误解析：** ten people 是复数，表示多个人的生命用 lives。

### 五、改写句子

**2.** A̲b̲o̲u̲t̲ ̲2̲2̲ ̲s̲t̲u̲d̲e̲n̲t̲s̲ in our class get to school before 7:00.（对划线部分提问）

   ______ ______ students in your class get to school before 7:00?

**朵朵原答案：** What do

**正确答案：** How many students in your class get to school before 7:00?

**错误解析：** 对人数提问用 How many；原句主语是 students，疑问句中不需要 do。

**3.** My favourite subject is S̲c̲i̲e̲n̲c̲e̲.（对划线部分提问）

   ______ ______ your favourite subject?

**朵朵原答案：** which is

**正确答案：** What is your favourite subject?

**错误解析：** 对具体学科名称提问用 What；主语由 my 改为 your，be 动词 is 保留。

**5.** Ms Wang, lab, is, experiments, the, in, doing, some（连词成句）

   ____________________________________________________________

**朵朵原答案：** Ms Wang is doing some experiment in the lab.

**正确答案：** Ms Wang is doing some experiments in the lab.

**错误解析：** 题目给出的 experiments 是复数，some 后接可数名词复数，不能写成 experiment。
